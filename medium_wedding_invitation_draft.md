## What Makes a Great Wedding Invitation?

A wedding invitation is more than just a card — it's the first impression your guests receive of your big day. It sets expectations, builds excitement, and (let's be honest) ends up on everyone's fridge or WhatsApp status for weeks. Whether you're planning a grand ballroom affair, an intimate garden ceremony, or a destination wedding by the beach, your invitation should reflect your unique story as a couple.

The best invitations manage to feel both personal and polished — elegant enough to frame, warm enough to share.

Traditionally, getting a quality wedding invitation meant choosing between three frustrating paths:

- **Last-minute WhatsApp forwards** — you send a blurry, cropped screenshot that looks nothing like your actual event, and you cringe every time you see it shared in the group chat.

None of these are deal-breakers. But all of them add stress to a period that's already full of it.
